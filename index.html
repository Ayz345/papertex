1 Introduction

The integral of a Gaussian over the whole real line has a closed form, which we state in equation (1) and prove in Section 2.

Editing
∞ ∫ −∞ e−x2 dx = √π (1)

Equation 1: the integral of e to the minus x squared, from minus infinity to infinity, equals the square root of pi.

Squaring both sides turns the problem into a double integral over the plane, which polar coordinates make elementary.

How it works

    
The document is the truth. LaTeX is the output.

    
    
      
    2. 
      
  2. 
        1
        
    Write on the page
    
        
    Headings, equations, figures, tables and citations are edited directly on a sheet set in Computer Modern — exactly what the PDF will look like. Know LaTeX already? Type it in the pane beside the page, or paste it in: the page follows.
    
      
    3. 
      
  3. 
        2
        
    The LaTeX writes itself
    
        
    A clean .tex file is generated from what you do — labels, environments and references, the way you would write them by hand.
    
      
    4. 
      
  4. 
        3
        
    Compile, or take it anywhere
    
        
    Typeset with real TeX Live, running in your browser. Or hand the source to Overleaf, a journal, or your own TeX installation — and bring a .tex or a whole Overleaf project back in to keep editing it here.

Nothing is uploaded

        
Editing, exporting and compiling all happen in your browser. When you compile, the engine is downloaded to you — your document isn’t sent anywhere.

      

      

        
        
No account, no cookies, no tracking

        
PaperTeX doesn’t know who you are. Open the page and start writing.

      

      

        
        
Keep a copy that lasts

        
Work lives in your browser’s storage, which clean-ups can erase — and Safari deletes it after 7 days without a visit. Save project writes a file to your disk that nothing can touch.
