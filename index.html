1 Introduction

The integral of a Gaussian over the whole real line has a closed form,

∫−∞∞ e−x² dx = √π(1)

which we prove in Section 2.

\begin{equation}
  \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^{2}}\,dx = \sqrt{\pi}
  \label{eq:gauss}
\end{equation}
