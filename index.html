1 Introduction

The integral of a Gaussian over the whole real line has a closed form, which we state in equation (1) and prove in Section 2.

Editing
∞ ∫ −∞ e−x2 dx = √π (1)

Equation 1: the integral of e to the minus x squared, from minus infinity to infinity, equals the square root of pi.

Squaring both sides turns the problem into a double integral over the plane, which polar coordinates make elementary.

How it works

    
The document is the truth. LaTeX is the output.

Nothing is uploaded

        
Editing, exporting and compiling all happen in your browser. When you compile, the engine is downloaded to you — your document isn’t sent anywhere.

      

      

        
        
No account, no cookies, no tracking

        
PaperTeX doesn’t know who you are. Open the page and start writing.

      

      

        
        
Keep a copy that lasts

        
Work lives in your browser’s storage, which clean-ups can erase. Save project writes a file to your disk that nothing can touch.
