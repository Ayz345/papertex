# Scoring sheet

I scored all twelve final documents (four testers × three rounds) against the same 27-item checklist. Each score comes from the LaTeX the editor generated for that document; the documents were also compiled. The `.tex`, the raw saved document (`.json`) and the compiled PDF for each are in [results/](results/).

**Scale:**
- **1:** the element is there and matches the original.
- **0.5:** it is there but visibly differs, or is only partly done.
- **0:** it is missing, or it is plain text where structure was needed.

**General rules:**
- Trailing punctuation after a display equation is ignored, unless it ends up in the wrong place (inside a root or a matrix).
- Theorem number style and the bibliography's final full stop come from the product's defaults, not from the tester, so they aren't scored.

**Caveats:**
- I wrote the checklist after seeing the round-1 documents, and I am the only scorer. The reason for every score below 1 is given in the notes, so each one can be checked.
- Round-1 LaTeX was regenerated by the current `latex.js` when the documents were archived. That doesn't change any item scored here, except that it makes round 1's D fail to compile (see the bottom of this sheet).
