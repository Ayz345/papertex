About /

PAPERTEX. You write on the page. It writes the LaTeX.

PaperTeX is a free LaTeX editor that runs entirely in your browser. Headings, equations, tables and references are edited directly on a page that looks like the finished PDF, and a clean, compilable .tex file is written for you as you go. Type int and you get an integral with the cursor in its lower limit; alpha becomes α; x1 becomes x₁. When you are done, PaperTeX compiles the document with real LaTeX (TeX Live, running in your browser) or hands you the source for Overleaf, a journal, or your own TeX installation.

It works the other way round too. Open a .tex file or a whole Overleaf project (its .zip, with the images and the .bib), paste LaTeX onto the page, or type it in the LaTeX pane beside the page: it becomes a document you can edit on the page.

Why /

LaTeX is an undeniable cornerstone of science. Papers, theses, lecture notes and lab reports are written in it, because it is how mathematics gets typeset: equations that number themselves, references that stay right when a section moves, and a source file that journals accept.

But LaTeX is a programming language. To put a fraction on a page you write code, compile it, and read an error log when a brace is missing. People with something to say about physics or mathematics spend their first weeks learning the tool instead of saying it, and some settle for a word processor and lose what LaTeX would have given them.

One shouldn’t have to code to create a document. PaperTeX keeps what makes LaTeX worth having and takes the code out of the way. You write on a page that looks like the finished paper, and the LaTeX is written for you. It is still yours: read it, edit it, or take it anywhere LaTeX goes.

Privacy /

  • Nothing is uploaded. Your documents never leave your computer. Editing, exporting and compiling all happen in your browser.
  • No account, no cookies, no tracking. PaperTeX does not know who you are.
  • Where your documents live: in your browser's storage for this site. Clearing the site's data deletes them, and Safari removes a site's storage after seven days without a visit. Use Save project to keep a copy of anything that matters — it is a file on your disk that no browser clean-up can touch.
  • When you compile, the LaTeX engine and its packages are downloaded from PaperTeX's own servers and cached by your browser. Your document itself is not sent anywhere.
