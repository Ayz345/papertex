PaperTeX

You write on the page. It writes the LaTeX.

PaperTeX is a free LaTeX editor that runs entirely in your browser. Headings, equations, tables and references are edited directly on a page that looks like the finished PDF, and a clean, compilable .tex file is written for you as you go. Type int and you get an integral with the caret in its lower limit; alpha becomes α; x1 becomes x₁. When you are done, PaperTeX compiles the document with real LaTeX (TeX Live, running in your browser) or hands you the source for Overleaf, a journal, or your own TeX installation.

Privacy

  • Nothing is uploaded. Your documents never leave your computer. Editing, exporting and compiling all happen in your browser.
  • No account, no cookies, no tracking. PaperTeX does not know who you are.
  • Where your documents live: in your browser's storage for this site. Clearing the site's data deletes them, and Safari removes a site's storage after seven days without a visit. Use Save project to keep a copy of anything that matters — it is a file on your disk that no browser clean-up can touch.
  • When you compile, the LaTeX engine and its packages are downloaded from PaperTeX's own servers and cached by your browser. Your document itself is not sent anywhere.
